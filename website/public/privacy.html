What we collect

The only information this website collects is what you type into the booking request form. That is:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number
  • Your email address, if you choose to give one — the field is optional
  • The style you are asking for
  • Your preferred date and time of day
  • Anything you write in the notes field

If you reached us by clicking an advertisement or a link, we also record which advertisement or link it was, and store that alongside your booking: the page you arrived on, any advert tags in its address, and the main address of the website that sent you (for example, facebook.com, not the page on it). It tells us which of our ads actually bring people in. If you have accepted cookies, Meta and Google Analytics are separately told that a booking request was submitted and which style it was for (for example, "Knotless Braids"). This website does not pass them your name, phone number, email address, notes or the date you asked for. See Cookies and tracking.

There is no account to create, no newsletter sign-up and no payment taken on this website. We do not ask for, and have no way to receive, your card details through this site.

Please do not put medical information in the notes field. If a scalp or skin condition affects your appointment, tell us in person or by phone instead.

How we use it

We use what you send for one purpose: arranging your appointment.

  • To contact you and confirm your slot, normally by text
  • To prepare for the style you have asked for
  • To reach you if something about your appointment has to change

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone for marketing, and we do not send you marketing messages you did not ask for.

Where it is stored

This website is a static site with no server of its own. Booking requests are sent to and stored in a database hosted by Supabase, our data processor, and the salon is notified when a new request arrives. Access to that database is restricted: the website itself can add a new request and cannot read any request back.

Cookies and tracking

Analytics and advertising tracking on this site are optional and need your consent. The first time you visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies from two tools: Google Analytics and the Meta (Facebook) pixel. Neither tool is loaded, and neither sets a cookie, unless you choose Accept. Until you answer, this website does not contact Facebook or Google Analytics.

If you say no

Neither tool is loaded and no tracking cookie is set, and the booking form and the rest of the website work the same way. We remember your answer so you are not asked again on every page; that memory is kept in your own browser. If we add a tool or materially change how these tools are used, we ask you again.

If you say yes

We load both tools. The Meta pixel sets Facebook cookies and tells Meta which pages you visit and which styles you look at; when you tap a booking button, start filling in the booking form, or tap to call or email us; and when you submit a booking request, with the style it was for.

Google Analytics sets Google Analytics cookies and tells Google which pages you view, which website or advert sent you, your approximate location (such as your town or city) and the type of device and browser you use, how far you scroll, which links to other websites you tap, and the same booking, call and email actions described above, including the style.

Both tools also receive the standard technical information any website request carries, such as your IP address. Before either tool loads, this website shortens the page address to the page itself and any advert tags in it, and reduces the website that sent you to its main address. That way, personal details that may be included in a link you followed are not passed on. Meta can still see its own advert click identifier, which it uses to tell which of its adverts you came from.

We use these tools for two purposes: to find out which of our adverts actually bring clients in, so we are not paying for adverts that do not work, and to see which pages people find useful. Google Analytics is set up with Google's advertising features and cross-device signals turned off, and we do not currently use Google Ads.

What this website does not pass to either tool: your name, your phone number, your email address, your notes, or the date you asked for. The tools learn what was done on the site and which style it concerned, not who you are. Meta and Google handle what they receive under their own privacy policies.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which removes your saved answer and these cookies together. You can also turn off ad personalisation in your own Facebook and Instagram settings, and opt out of Google Analytics across all sites with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

What runs regardless

If you arrive from an advertisement or a link, your browser holds on to which one it was for the length of that visit, so the detail is still there if you reach the booking form. That is kept in your browser's session storage, not a cookie; it is erased when you close the tab; no other website can read it; and it is only ever sent to us if you actually submit a booking. Only the page you arrived on, any advert tags in its address and the main address of the website that sent you are kept. It happens whether or not you accept cookies, because it is how we know which advert to credit — and it involves no third party.

Third-party services

These third parties are involved in running this site, and each can see technical information such as your IP address as a normal part of delivering it:

  • Supabase — stores booking requests, as described above.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this site are loaded from Google's font servers, so Google receives the request for those files.
  • Meta (Facebook) — only if you accept cookies. See Cookies and tracking above. If you decline, or have not answered, this website does not load the Meta pixel or send anything to Meta.
  • Google Analytics — only if you accept cookies, on the same terms as Meta above. If you decline, or have not answered, this website does not load Google Analytics or send anything to it.

Our website host also keeps standard server logs of requests made to the site.

How long we keep it

We keep booking requests for as long as we need them to run the salon and to keep a record of appointments, and in any case no longer than three years after your most recent appointment. After that they are deleted.

Your choices

Write to booking@mimi-african-braiding-styling.com or call 571-426-0602 and you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your details from our records
  • Stop contacting you

You can reply STOP to any text message from us and we will stop texting you.

Children

We braid children's hair, but this website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from a child. A booking for a child should be made by their parent or guardian, using the parent's or guardian's contact details.
