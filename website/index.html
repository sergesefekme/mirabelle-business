01 — Who We Are

Mirabelle.B, in Ashburn

A braiding atelier in Loudoun County, working in braids, locs and microlocs, wigs and sew-ins. One chair, one standard, and a finish time we tell you before you sit down.

Meet Mirabelle

Mirabelle's passion for African hair braiding began with a love for beauty, creativity, and helping women feel confident. For her, braiding is more than just a hairstyle — it is an art, a tradition, and a way to bring out the beauty in every client.

At Mirabelle.B, she takes the time to understand each client's style and creates beautiful, neat, and comfortable braids with care and attention to detail. Her goal is simple: every client should leave her chair feeling beautiful, confident, and happy.

01

The Hands

Mirabelle brings more than 7 years of experience in African hair braiding, combining traditional techniques with modern protective styles. Every client receives personal attention from consultation through the final braid.

02

Protective work first

Braids, cornrows, twists and locs are load-bearing, not decorative. Tension is set at the scalp, parts are measured, and nothing is installed tighter than your hairline can carry.

03

A clean chair

Tools are cleaned between clients and the station is reset for every appointment. Children and tender-headed clients are told what is happening before it happens.

04

Who we serve

Ashburn, Sterling, Leesburg, Herndon and the wider Northern Virginia area. Appointments and walk-ins, Monday to Saturday.
